Optional Google connection

Your library,
across devices.

Connect the same Google account on Mac and iPad to synchronize PDFs, annotations, notes and library organization through your own Google Drive storage.

Google Sign-In identifies your account. Alexandria uses your account identifier, name and email to show the connected account and keep its library separate.

Drive access is limited to Alexandria’s app storage. The app reads and writes its own hidden application-data folder. It does not request access to browse the rest of your Drive.

Sync runs while Alexandria is open and active. Download PDFs when you need them, or use Keep Offline to retain them on a device. You can also use a local library without connecting Google.

This beta does not provide live collaboration or guaranteed background sync. Keep independent copies of important documents.

An optional Mac feature

Ask about a paper.

Mac builds include experimental AI chat using your own OpenRouter API key and selected model. Sending a question can share document text, notes, annotations and page images as context with OpenRouter and the model provider.

AI chat is separate from Google sign-in and sync. It is optional, may incur provider charges, and its answers should be checked against the source. Read the AI data details.
